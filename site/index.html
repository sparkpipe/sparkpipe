How it works

Four steps

Account, hours, reservation, use.

  1. Open an account

    Give your company's details and connect an Ethereum wallet: its address identifies the account. Accept the Hourly Terms by signing their text with the wallet. Every business is screened against sanctions lists.

  2. Buy hours

    Pay in advance: Korean businesses in won, by bank transfer against our electronic tax invoice; customers abroad in USDC, through an invoice from our payment processor. Korean VAT of 10% is added. Your hours are issued once the payment has cleared.

  3. Reserve

    Choose a fleet, a model and the hours on the fleet's calendar, and sign; on hourly terms you reserve up to 7 days ahead. A confirmed reservation is final and is taken from your balance as the hour passes.

  4. Use

    During your hours the whole fleet serves only your requests, through the SparkPipe API, which follows the OpenAI chat format. You issue the access tokens, so you decide who uses your hours.

Won payments go straight to HYOR AI Tech Ltd.'s Korean bank account. USDC payments go through a payment processor. Our system has no feature for passing hours between accounts.

Technology

Open source

Sixteen small boxes, one frontier model.

Decoding a large model is limited by how fast its weights can be read. SparkPipe splits every weight and every cache entry across the sixteen nodes, so each reads only its own sixteenth, then merges the partial results over a 100 Gb/s RDMA mesh in 16 to 38 microseconds. The engine is written from scratch in C and CUDA and published under the Apache 2.0 license.
