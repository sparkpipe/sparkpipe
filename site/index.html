Why

A fleet of your own

Built for developers who live in a large codebase.

Your context stays warm

Shared APIs drop your prompt cache after minutes. On a fleet pinned to you, the cached state of your requests stays from one request to the next and from one week to the next, so long contexts answer in seconds, not minutes. It is deleted only when another customer's booking of that fleet starts, or 24 hours after your last hour on it.

Your files stay too

Every fleet has 4 TB of storage for you: a mirror of your repository, datasets, adapters and models. Export it and restore it to any fleet you hold, whenever you like.

Functions on your own data

Run our functions on your fleet in your own hours: train a draft model on your code and your sessions overnight, so speculation accepts more of the model's tokens the next day, prepare your cache, or review your code. What they produce is yours.

Nobody else's requests

A pinned fleet serves only you: no shared queue, no shared cache, no rate limits but the fleet's own speed. Keep it as long as you keep booking the next week.

Only in Korea

Your requests and data are processed only on fleets and servers we operate ourselves in Korea, and are never sent to any other computing or AI service. You contract with a Korean company under Korean law.

How it works

Four steps

Demo hour, then a fleet of your own.

  1. Open an account

    Give your company's legal name, or your own name, and a work email, confirm it with a 6-digit code, and accept the SparkPipe Customer Agreement. No password and no crypto wallet.

  2. Book a demo hour

    Buy HC and book the whole demo fleet by the hour, up to 7 days ahead, 1 HC an hour. Point your own tools at it and see your codebase on a fleet that serves only you.

  3. Pin a fleet

    Book your first week on a free fleet, from the next Monday. When every fleet is taken, join the waitlist by locking at least 144 HC: the biggest lock goes first, fleets are allocated every Sunday at 00:00 KST, and your lock books your weeks the moment your turn comes. You see your own position.

  4. Keep it

    Book the next week before 00:00 on the Sunday of your last booked week and the fleet stays yours: nobody else can book any of its hours. Hold more weeks ahead and each new week costs less. If you stop, the fleet goes to the waitlist that Sunday, and your cache and files are deleted when the next customer starts on Monday.

Weeks run Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00 KST. Sunday, the last day of every week, is the free one. Won payments go through Toss Payments and USDC and USDT payments through CoinPayments; HYOR AI Tech Ltd. stays the seller.

Your tools

OpenAI-compatible

Point your own tools at your fleet.

Your fleet speaks the OpenAI chat-completions API. Create an API key in your account and set it in any coding agent, editor or SDK that lets you choose an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Requests go to the fleet you hold at that moment; the fleet loads the model you name if it is not already loaded.

# your fleet, while you hold it
export OPENAI_BASE_URL=https://sparkpipe.ai/pay/api/v1
export OPENAI_API_KEY=your key from your account

curl $OPENAI_BASE_URL/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENAI_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model": "kimi-k3", "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}]}'

Holding two fleets? Name one with "sparkpipe": {"fleet": "F2"}. A long prompt can ask for a prefill accelerator with "sparkpipe": {"accelerator": true}.

Technology

Open source

Sixteen small boxes, one frontier model.

Decoding a large model is limited by how fast its weights can be read. SparkPipe splits every weight and every cache entry across the sixteen nodes, so each reads only its own sixteenth, then merges the partial results over a 100 Gb/s RDMA mesh in 16 to 38 microseconds. The engine is written from scratch in C and CUDA and published under the Apache 2.0 license.
