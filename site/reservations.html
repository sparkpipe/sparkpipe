Reservations

Reserve time on the fleet.

Sixteen DGX Sparks run as one machine. Reserve a dedicated window and your workload gets the whole cluster — weights staged, engines warm, no queue between you and the tokens.

How it works

  1. Pay for your reserved window below — cryptocurrency or Bitcoin, processed by NOWPayments.
  2. Send the payment ID from your receipt to hyor@sparkpipe.ai with the workload description and your preferred window (UTC).
  3. We confirm the slot, stage the model, and hand you an OpenAI-compatible endpoint for the duration.

Rates and window sizes are set per workload; write first if you need a quote before paying.
