Fleets

Set up your fleets

Every fleet starts each booking with only our base image and your own working state on it, if any. For each fleet you hold or have booked, choose what to install on it from the Catalogue, or nothing. An item whose license asks for acceptance is chosen only with your acceptance of that license for your company. Your choice stays with your working state on that fleet. Installing software and loading a model count as part of your booking.

What happens next

  • Each purchase of HC lapses 12 months after it is issued unless you book with it, and the HC that lapses first is spent first. Your account shows every lot.
  • In your account, book hours on an Hour Fleet from 1 December 2026 and weeks on a fleet pinned to you from 4 January 2027, or join the waitlist. No booking is ever bumped.
  • Auto-extend is on for every fleet pinned to you: at the start of your last booked week it books the next one from your unlocked HC, so a missed Sunday never costs you the fleet. Keep more weeks ahead, or turn it off with one click, under Fleets in your account. We email you 72, 48, 6 and 2 hours before a deadline while the next week is not booked.
  • Receipts go to . A card payment's card slip is its VAT document; for a bank transfer or virtual account in won we issue an electronic tax invoice (세금계산서) to the Korean business registration number you gave; a USD bank transfer (SWIFT) carries 0% VAT, shown on the invoice we email. For anything else, write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai.

USD bank transfers (SWIFT) to make

Send each amount by SWIFT from your company's own account outside Korea, with its payment reference in the payment details and charges OUR, so that the whole amount arrives. Your HC is issued once the whole amount reaches our account. VAT is 0% on these transfers.
