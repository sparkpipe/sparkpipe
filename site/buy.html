Questions

Before you pay

Good to know.

Do I need a crypto wallet extension?

No. Your account is your email. You pay on CoinPayments' checkout page from any wallet or exchange account that can send USDC: copy the address, or scan the QR code with your phone.

Which network should I choose?

The one your USDC is on. Base and Solana have the lowest fees; Ethereum the highest. Send only USDC, only on the network you chose: the checkout page shows the exact amount and address for it.

When can I use the hours?

From the first day you choose, at the earliest 1 December 2026, for 12 months. You reserve hours on a fleet's calendar up to 7 days ahead; we email you when the calendar opens for your account.

How many hours can I hold?

Up to 168 hours at a time, counting unused hours and unpaid orders, or 720 once we have checked your business. Write to us for the check.

Why is VAT added?

We are a Korean company, and Korean VAT of 10% applies to every USDC payment (Hourly Terms, clause 3.2). The invoice shows it separately.

Can we pay in won against a tax invoice?

Yes. Korean businesses can pay by bank transfer against our electronic tax invoice (세금계산서): write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai.

What if I send the wrong amount, or too late?

A short or late payment issues no hours automatically. Write to us with your order number: we check it, then either issue the hours or refund the payment through CoinPayments.
