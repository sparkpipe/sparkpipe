Questions

Before you pay

Good to know.

Do I need a crypto wallet extension?

No. Your account is your email. You pay on CoinPayments' checkout page from any wallet or exchange account that can send USDC or USDT: copy the address, or scan the QR code with your phone.

Which coin and network should I choose?

The ones you hold. Each tile shows the name exchanges use on their withdrawal screens: ERC20, TRC20, BEP20, Base, SOL, Arbitrum One or Polygon PoS. Network fees are usually cents on all of them. Send only the coin you chose, only on the network you chose: the checkout page shows the exact amount and address for it. If your exchange takes a withdrawal fee from the amount, add it on top so the whole amount arrives.

Why stablecoins?

Prices are in US dollars, and dollar stablecoins keep their value between the invoice and the payment, so neither of us carries the price swings of other crypto. We list USDC and USDT, which carry almost all stablecoin payments; CoinPayments' checkout page also takes the other dollar stablecoins it offers.

When can I use my HC?

At once in the shared pool, and on our own fleet-hours from 1 December 2026, reserved in the Hour Market up to 7 days ahead at the fleet's Rate, or up to 31 days ahead at 1.25× on any day. Once confirmed, a reservation is yours: no one can bump it. Each purchase lasts 12 months from the day it is issued, and the oldest HC is spent first.

What do prefill accelerators cost?

A prefill accelerator computes a long prompt for a request that asks for one and hands it back to the fleet, which writes the reply; none is serving yet. Each prompt pays for its accelerator-seconds, from when the accelerator starts it until it hands it back, times the accelerator's Rate, times your queue bid, divided by 3,600. The Rate follows size against a 16-Spark fleet: 3 for 48 Sparks, 6 for 96. So 180 seconds at Rate 6 with a queue bid of 1.5 costs 180 × 6 × 1.5 ÷ 3,600 = 0.45 HC. The queue bid, from 1 to 10 in steps of 0.01, buys a better place in the queue: the highest is computed first, and each prompt pays its own bid. Accelerator time is charged in the shared pool, during your own reserved hours and under a plan alike, in HC or with Plan hours. A prompt the accelerator does not finish costs nothing, and one that no accelerator starts in time runs on its fleet without one. HC close to lapsing is well spent here: accelerator time takes the HC that lapses first, with no hour to reserve or release (Customer Agreement).

Can HC sell out?

Yes, on purpose. We sell HC only while our free fleet-hours can deliver it: before every date, the hours on our fleets that nobody holds, after those kept for plan holders and an allowance for maintenance, must cover all the HC that lapses by then. Beyond that a purchase is refused, and the Hour Market shows how much HC is still on sale. So every HC you buy can be spent (Customer Agreement).

Can I try it first?

Yes. The Hour Market sandbox runs the same market with no money: buy HC, reserve and release hours, bid, and call the pool against a simulator.

Why is VAT added?

We are a Korean company, and Korean VAT of 10% applies to every stablecoin payment (Customer Agreement). The invoice shows it separately.

Can we pay in won against a tax invoice?

Yes. Korean businesses can pay by bank transfer against our electronic tax invoice (세금계산서): write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai.

What if I send the wrong amount, or too late?

If less arrives than the invoice, the HC it covers appears in your account at once, in proportion to what arrived, to six decimals; buy more whenever you like. Anything sent above the invoice, or after the payment page has closed, CoinPayments refunds with an email link to claim it. No one needs to write to anyone.
