Fleet-hours · checkout

A whole fleet, by the hour.

One fleet-hour is an entire SparkPipe fleet for sixty minutes. Try it prepaid by the hour, or take every hour from Monday to Saturday of a two-year term at the lower price. Your hours start on the date you choose.

You pay in USDC, on any network our payment processor CoinPayments accepts, on its checkout page. You sign each order with your Ethereum wallet; signing moves no funds.

The Hourly Terms and the two-year agreement are published for review and come into force when sales open on 1 December 2026. How we handle customer data: privacy policy.

Prepaid hours for any business, with no business check: hold up to 168 hours at a time, or 720 (30 days) once we have approved your business. From Monday to Saturday, a two-year customer may take an hourly reservation for its guaranteed daily hours until 6 hours before it begins, and the hour comes back to you. Two-year

Monday to Saturday

Six days a week for two years, at the lower price.

For businesses we approve, signed individually after our business check: write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai. The term starts the day you sign and covers every hour of it from Monday to Saturday on one fleet's capacity: 15,000 to 15,048 hours, depending on the Sundays and 29 February in the term. Sundays are kept for our maintenance; you may still use your hours on a Sunday when a fleet is free. After signing you can pay any amount at any time, up to the whole term, and meet each deadline at your own pace. Hours exist only once paid; hours that pass before they are paid for are lost. Nothing is due in the first 30 days (Phase A), and if you pay nothing by then the agreement ends with nothing owed.
