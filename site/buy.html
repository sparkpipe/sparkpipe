Credits

Yours to trade

Your credits.

Hourly credits are for your own use. Two-year credits are released in full once prepayment completes, one class per month and window, such as 2027-03-E. Each class expires at the end of its month. Released credits are yours: transfer them, sell them, or swap East for West or one month for another. A sell or swap order is a signed message any orderbook can match; settling it is a signed transfer, recorded in the ledger.
