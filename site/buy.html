Hourly

No commitment

Try it by the hour.

Prepaid hours for any business, with no business check: hold up to 168 hours at a time, or 720 (30 days) once we have approved your business. A two-year customer may take an hourly reservation until 6 hours before it begins, and the hour comes back to you.

Korean businesses pay in won by bank transfer against our electronic tax invoice (세금계산서); others pay in USDC through an invoice. VAT is added; any VAT that turns out not to be owed is an overpayment, and we refund it. This preview shows dollar prices.

Two-year

Every hour of the term

Every hour of two years, at the lower price.

For businesses we approve. The two-year term starts the day you sign and covers every hour of it on one fleet's capacity: 17,520 hours, or 17,544 when it includes 29 February. After signing you can pay any amount at any time, up to the whole term, and meet each deadline at your own pace. Hours exist only once paid, and you choose when to use them; hours that pass before they are paid for are lost. Nothing is due in the first 30 days (Phase A), and if you pay nothing by then the agreement ends with nothing owed. Once the last gate is paid, every tranche is released: either window within your election, and spending ahead. The models you name in the agreement stay available for the whole term.

Business check: not approved

In the live service this is a manual review of company documents, owners and sanctions screening. Approval raises your hourly cap to 30 days of hours and opens the two-year agreement.

Ledger

Append-only

Every change is one line.

Each order, payment, issue of hours and reservation is appended to a hash-chained ledger: every line carries the hash of the one before it, so no line can change unnoticed. This is the demo's own copy.
