Catalogue

Updated 13 November 2026

The Catalogue is the software, including ours, that our website lists for you to install on a fleet you hold (Customer Agreement, clauses 1.1 and 7.1). This page is that list. Every fleet starts each Booking with only our base image and your own working state, if any (Fleet List). You choose what to install on it: an item below, nothing at all, or any other software you have the right to use. You operate what you install as your own, under its license, and run, stop and remove it as you decide (clauses 7.1 and 7.2). The Korean version (catalogue-ko.html) has the same content and prevails if they differ.

Our inference engine

Installing SparkPipe, our inference engine, on a fleet asks you to accept its license, the Functional Source License 1.1 (ALv2 future license) with an additional permission. You accept it for your company by ticking the box on your account page that names your company, SparkPipe, the fleet and the license, or by adding --accept-license to sparkpipe install, and the license then governs the copy installed on that fleet. We record the statement you accepted with the SHA-256 hash of each license text as we serve it, who accepted it, the time, your IP address and your browser or command-line tool, as our privacy policy states.

The engine runs the open-source models listed on the Fleet List. You obtain each model you choose from its publisher, under its license, into your Customer storage: we run no AI model for you (clauses 5.2 and 7.1). A model listed when you make a Booking stays runnable with Catalogue software for that Booking, and other models while they are listed.

The engine's source is public at github.com/sparkpipe/sparkpipe, under the same license, so you can read its code before you install it.

How to choose

Choose for each fleet you hold, or have a booking on that has not ended, on your account page under Set up your fleets, or with the SparkPipe command-line tool:

  • sparkpipe install lists your fleets and what you chose for each.
  • sparkpipe install F1B sparkpipe --accept-license chooses our engine for Fleet 1B and accepts its license for your company.
  • sparkpipe install F1B vllm installs vLLM from its publisher on Fleet 1B.
  • sparkpipe install F1B none keeps only the base image on Fleet 1B.

The account's owner or an admin chooses. Your choice stays with your working state on that fleet (clause 6.2): it carries over from one of your Bookings to the next, and ends when another customer's Booking of the fleet starts or 24 hours after your last hour on it. Then choose again for your next Booking there. Installing software and loading a model count as part of your Booking (clause 14.3).

Changes

We may add, change or remove items without amending the Customer Agreement, and a change does not alter software you have already installed (clause 7.1). Each item is under its own license, which you must comply with (clause 7.2). The Catalogue is listed “as available” (clause 8.4).
