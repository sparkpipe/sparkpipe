Model licenses

Each model is the publisher's open-weight software under its own license, which the customer must follow (Hourly Terms 8.1, agreement 10.3). The conditions of the offered models:

  • Kimi K3: a model-as-a-service business whose group revenue exceeds US$20 million in any 12 months needs a separate agreement with Moonshot AI, and a commercial product with more than 100 million monthly active users or US$20 million in monthly revenue must display "Kimi K3" in its interface.
  • GLM 5.3: a model-as-a-service business whose group revenue exceeds US$10 billion in any 12 months must pass Z.AI's security review before commercial use.
  • MIT (GLM 5.3 Flash, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, MiMo V2.6, Ling 3.0) and Apache 2.0 (Gemma 4, Muse Glimmer 30B, Hunyuan Hy4 Preview): no field-of-use or revenue condition.
  • OpenMDW 1.1 (Laguna S 2.1): no condition on use or outputs; the license ends for anyone who sues claiming the model infringes a patent or copyright.

The Kimi K3 and GLM-5.3 licenses also require every use to comply with applicable law. Every license requires the publisher's notices to stay with any copy of the weights; customers use the models on our fleets and receive no copy.

Limits of today's builds

  • Batches: Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3 serve at most 8 requests at once, GLM 5.3 Flash 16.
  • Speculation runs on Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3, for one request at a time. It speeds up text the request already contains, such as whole-file code rewrites (Kimi K3 15 to 126 tok/s, GLM 5.3 28 to 124 tok/s); on new prose the gain is 1.0 to 1.2 times.
  • Long prompts are slow from cold: prefill reaches 1 to 4% of the fleet's compute floor, so a 137,700-token prompt takes 299 s on Kimi K3. A follow-up turn that reuses the cached prefix takes about 5 s.
  • One model at a time holds a fleet. Loading another takes about a minute, up to 79 s for Kimi K3. A load inside a reserved hour counts as part of that hour (Hourly Terms 5.3).
