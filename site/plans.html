Reserved plans

Your fleets in your office hours, from ₩18,500 or $14 an hour.

Choose your office hours in your own time zone, the days, how many fleets and whether to lock them, and pay month by month or for a term of 1 to 3 years. A Monthly plan pays each month at the Monthly price shown when you pay for it, and stops whenever you like; a term fixes its Hour Price for the whole term. Term

Office hours

Whole hours of your local clock, at least 8 hours, following your clock's daylight-saving changes; an end at or before the start runs past midnight.

Days

Weekdays are Monday to Friday by the date where you are, on which your office hours start, public holidays included; Every day runs seven days a week, for long-running work.

Fleets

How many whole fleets you hold at once in those hours; more on request, subject to our approval.

Fleet lock

A locked fleet is the same named fleet as long as the plan runs and yours alone, never offered to anyone else, so your model state and caches stay on it instead of being cleared for another customer. Another customer uses it only for an hour you release, and we clear it first.
