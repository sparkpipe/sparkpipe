Build a plan

Five choices and a start date.

The term starts on that day and ends the day before the same date at the end of the term.

Term

The longer the term, the lower its Hour Price, fixed for the whole term.

Windows

Each window is 8 hours of the Korean day, the working day of its region; choose any one or more, and all three is a Full day.

Days

Every day runs seven days a week, for long-running work; Weekdays runs Monday to Friday in each window's own region, so the Americas Window runs on KST Tuesday to Saturday.

Fleets

How many whole fleets you hold at once in those hours; more on request, subject to our approval.

Fleet lock

A locked fleet is the same named fleet for the whole term and yours alone, never offered to anyone else, so your model state and caches stay on it instead of being cleared for another customer. Another customer uses it only for an hour you release, and we clear it first.
