Hour Market · livesandbox

Hour-credits for whole AI fleets.

One hour-credit (HC) buys one hour of a Rate-1 fleet and lasts 12 months. Spend it per request in the shared pool, reserve a whole fleet by the hour, or bid for the windows you need. Release a reserved hour you won't use, and get HC back for what it earns, less a 10% fee. The Hour Market Rules and the Hourly Terms apply.

LiveSandbox, no money

Real accounts and real payments. Try every flow first in the sandbox.

The same market with its own accounts: purchases settle at once with no money, sign-in codes show on screen, the shared test clock moves time forward, and the pool answers with a simulator instead of the fleets.

Times are Korea Standard Time. Our own fleet-hours can be reserved from 1 December 2026: at Rate up to 7 days ahead, and Monday to Saturday up to 31 days ahead at 1.25×. A reservation is never bumped.
