Book weeks

Pin an available fleet from the next Monday, or extend a fleet pinned to you from the end of your last booked week. Each week runs Monday 00:00 to Sunday 24:00 KST, and Sunday is the free day. The discount counts the weeks you hold on that fleet, including the current week and the ones you are booking.

Waitlist

Lock at least 144 HC to join. Every Sunday at 00:00 KST, each fleet that comes free goes to the biggest lock, and at equal locks to whoever joined first. The whole lock then books consecutive weeks on that fleet from the next Monday, at the discount for that many weeks, and any HC left over is unlocked. Locked HC cannot be spent; add to your lock or leave whenever you like. You see your own position and how many locks are waiting, nothing more. Locked HC that would lapse while you wait is refunded.

Use your fleet

Your fleet speaks the Anthropic Messages API for Claude Code, the OpenAI Responses API for Codex, and OpenAI chat completions for Cursor and other OpenAI-compatible tools. Requests go to the fleet you hold at that moment, and the fleet loads the model you name if it is not already loaded. Holding two fleets? Name one with "sparkpipe": {"fleet": "F3"}.
