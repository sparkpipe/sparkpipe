Key terms
  • For business, professional, commercial or independent research and development use, not as a household consumer.
  • Prepaid: one HC costs ₩26,000 when paid in won, otherwise $20, plus 10% VAT, and buys one hour of a Rate-1 fleet or seat-seconds through the API. HC is issued once the payment completes.
  • Each purchase lasts 12 months from issue, and the HC that lapses first is spent first.
  • HC has no cash value, cannot be moved to another account and is not refunded, unless we stop offering service for HC, close your account other than for your breach, or the law requires a refund.
  • A booking is final and never taken from you. You can sell an hour back before it starts and get HC for what it earns, less a 10% fee.
  • A booked hour that is down for more than 15 consecutive minutes is credited back in full, automatically.
  • Requests and data are processed only on fleets, accelerators and servers we operate in Korea, and go to no other computing or AI service. Transient caches are deleted within 24 hours.
  • Our liability for claims about HC is capped at what you paid us for HC in the 6 months before the event giving rise to the claim.
  • Korean law; disputes go to arbitration in Seoul under the Korean Commercial Arbitration Board's rules.

The Customer Agreement and the Hour Market Rules govern, and the Korean text prevails.

Prefill accelerators

Our own DGX Spark machines in Seoul, shared by every fleet, that compute a long prompt and hand it back to its fleet to write the reply. Ask for one per request with "sparkpipe": {"accelerator": {"bid": 1}}. A prompt costs its accelerator-seconds times the accelerator's Rate times its priority. Each Rate follows the accelerator's computing capacity against a Rate-1 fleet, Rate 3 for one of 48 DGX Sparks, so at Standard you pay for about the same Spark-time as on your own fleet, finished sooner. Priority buys a better place in the queue: Standard ×1, Priority ×2, Rush ×5; the API takes any bid from 1 to , and the highest goes first. You pay in HC, from the HC that lapses first, or with this month's plan hours ("pay": "plan"), during your own booked hour too. A prompt that no accelerator starts within maxWait seconds (default , at most ) runs on its fleet without one and pays nothing for an accelerator, unless you send "fallback": false.
