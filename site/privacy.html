Privacy

Effective 31 October 2026 · 국문 개인정보처리방침 (the Korean text governs)

This policy explains how HYOR AI Tech Ltd. (효르에이아이테크 유한회사, business registration 641-87-03809, Yongsan-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea; "we") handles personal information. It covers the sparkpipe.ai and hyor.io websites, SparkPipe Chat at sparkpipe.ai:8443, fleet-hour customer accounts and the requests sent during their hours, the Hour Market and the shared pool, and email sent to us. It is published under Article 30 of Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.

1. The short version

  • The models run on computers we own and operate in Seoul. We do not send your conversations to any other AI company, and we do not train models on them.
  • We do not sell personal information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • We keep each kind of data only as long as the table below says, then delete it.
  • You can ask us at any time to see, correct, delete or stop processing your data: write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai.

2. Where your data is processed

The models run on sixteen NVIDIA DGX Spark computers and a server that hosts the chat application. We operate all of them in Seoul on SK Telecom's network. Public traffic reaches them through a server we rent from KCInfra in Seoul, which ends the encrypted HTTPS connection, forwards traffic to our hardware through an encrypted tunnel, and runs our email. Code you run with the chat's code interpreter runs in your own browser. Speech-to-text, document reading and search over your files run on our own hardware.

The people who operate SparkPipe can access stored accounts, chats, chat memory and logs. They do so only to run the service, give support, keep it secure or meet a legal obligation.

4. Deletion

When a retention period ends or its purpose is met, we delete the data from our systems so it cannot be recovered, and destroy any printed copy by shredding. Where a law requires us to keep data longer (the 3-month access logs and the 5-year tax records above), we keep only those items, separately, and only for that period.

5. Giving data to third parties

We do not give personal information to third parties, except where a law requires it, such as a court order or a warrant (PIPA Art. 17 and 18).

8. Your rights

You can view, export and delete your chats in the chat application. You may also ask us to show you your personal information, correct or delete it, or stop processing it, and you may withdraw any consent (PIPA Art. 35 to 37). A legal representative or someone you authorise may ask for you. Write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai; we answer within 10 days. We may decline only where a law allows it, for example when a law requires us to keep the data, and we will tell you why. No decision about you is made solely by automated processing (Art. 37-2): our sanctions screening only flags accounts for a person to review. Depending on where you live, for example in the EU or the UK, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority.

9. How we protect data

  • All traffic to sparkpipe.ai and hyor.io is encrypted with HTTPS; traffic between our servers runs through encrypted tunnels.
  • Passwords and shared-pool API keys are stored only as hashes, and account data is kept on our server in Seoul, readable only by the payment service and its administrators.
  • Only the people who operate SparkPipe can reach the systems, by personal key. Servers sit behind firewalls that block addresses after repeated failed logins.
  • We keep access records for our systems and review them after any incident.
  • Our computers are in premises we control in Seoul, and our public server is in KCInfra's data centre.

No system is perfectly secure, so do not send information you could not afford to have exposed.

10. Cookies and browser storage

The chat keeps you signed in with a session token stored in your browser; without it the chat cannot work. The fleet-hour account keeps you signed in with the cookie sp_session for 30 days: it holds only your account number, its expiry and our signature over them, and is sent only to our payment service. The checkout remembers the hours you chose in the browser's session storage until you pay. None of these leaves your browser except to sign you in.

Language. On your first visit, the site shows Korean pages if your IP address is in the Korean address ranges that APNIC, the regional internet registry, publishes, and English pages otherwise. The check runs on our own server against a copy of that list; your address goes to no one else, and we keep no record of the result. When you choose a language with the EN / 한국어 switch, a cookie named lang, holding only ko or en, remembers your choice for one year and is sent to our server with each page request.

Chat controls and local mode. The chat keeps your language choice, the memory switch of each chat and the compaction setting in the browser's local storage. In local mode (sparkpipe.ai:8443/local/) your chats are stored only in the browser's IndexedDB: each message is sent to the model and the reply comes back, and our servers keep no chat history or memory of them.

We use no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. You can delete or block any of these in your browser settings.

11. Children

SparkPipe is not meant for children under 16; please do not create an account if you are under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information of children under 14, which PIPA Article 22-2 allows only with a legal guardian's consent. If we learn that we have, we delete it.
