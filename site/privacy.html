Privacy

Effective 24 October 2026

This policy covers the sparkpipe.ai website, SparkPipe Chat at sparkpipe.ai:8443 and the SparkPipe API at sparkpipe.ai/v1. They are operated by HYOR AI Tech Ltd ("we"), Yongsan-gu, Seoul, South Korea. Write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai with any question or request about your data.

The short version

  • The models run on hardware we own and operate. We do not send your conversations to any other AI company.
  • We store your chats so the chat works. People who operate SparkPipe can access them.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking. We do not train models on your conversations.
  • You can ask us at any time to export or delete your data.

Where your data is processed

The models run on sixteen NVIDIA DGX Spark computers and a server that hosts the chat application. We operate all of them in Seoul, South Korea, on SK Telecom's network. Public traffic reaches them through a server we rent from WorldStream in the Netherlands. That server ends the encrypted HTTPS connection and forwards traffic to our hardware in Seoul through an encrypted tunnel. If you are in the EU, your data is therefore transferred to South Korea, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection.

When a third party is involved

  • Web search in the chat. When you turn on web search, the search query goes from our own search server to public search engines. It is sent from our server's address and without your account details. Pages from the results are fetched by our server.
  • Tools. When the model uses a tool such as the GitHub tool, the tool's request goes to that service.
  • Fonts on the website. The sparkpipe.ai home, playground and provider pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it loads them. See Google's privacy policy. The chat, the API and this page do not use Google Fonts.
  • Payments. Payments are made in cryptocurrency through CoinPayments, on CoinPayments' own pages. CoinPayments processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your wallet credentials or private keys.
  • Hosting, network and certificates. WorldStream hosts our public server in the Netherlands, and SK Telecom carries traffic to our hardware in Seoul. Traffic between the two is encrypted. Let's Encrypt issues our HTTPS certificate and receives no user data.

Code that you run with the chat's code interpreter runs inside your own browser. Speech-to-text, document reading and search over your files run on our own hardware.

We share personal data only with the parties above, or when the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

The chat keeps you signed in with a session token stored in your browser. The playground stores the API address you enter in your browser's local storage, and your API key in session storage, which clears when you close the tab. Neither leaves your browser except in the requests you make. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

Who can see your data

The people who operate SparkPipe can access stored accounts, chats, chat memory and logs. They do so only to run the service, give support, keep it secure or meet a legal obligation.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and export your chats in the chat application, and delete them there.
  • Email hyor@sparkpipe.ai to get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account together with its chats, chat memory and cached state. We answer within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, for example in the EU or the UK, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority.

Security

All traffic to sparkpipe.ai is encrypted with HTTPS, and traffic between the public server and our hardware runs through an encrypted tunnel. Access to our systems is limited to the people who operate them. No system is perfectly secure, so do not send information you could not afford to have exposed.

Children

SparkPipe is not meant for children under 16. Please do not create an account if you are under 16.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the effective date above. We announce material changes in the chat.
