How we measure

  • Every 30 seconds the gateway at sparkpipe.ai asks each fleet over its tunnel whether every loaded model answers. A fleet is down from its last good answer until its next one, so a period always starts a little early and ends a little late. Periods that only a single good answer separates count as one period for the credit, and each of them is marked creditable when that period is.
  • A time in which the gateway itself was not running, or could not ask, counts as down too: you could not reach the fleet then either.
  • A period over 15 minutes is credited as soon as it passes 15 minutes, again each day it lasts, and when it ends, each booked hour once. Hours under announced maintenance are credited as maintenance instead (clause 4.3), and uptime here leaves them out.
  • If your records show such a period and ours do not, send them to hyor@sparkpipe.ai within 7 days, and we credit the hours unless our records show the fleet was available throughout.
  • The same figures are at https://sparkpipe.ai/pay/api/status as JSON.
