Security

Published 5 November 2026

A fleet you hold serves only you. This page explains how we keep it that way: what runs on it, what happens when it changes hands, what we record, and who can reach the machines. The Customer Agreement, our data processing agreement and our privacy policy are the binding texts; where this page and they differ, they govern.

1. One customer per fleet

  • A fleet you hold runs only your requests. While your booking lasts you have the exclusive use of the whole fleet (Agreement clause 5.1): no other customer's requests, cache or code run on it, and there is no shared queue.
  • Your cache and storage stay on that fleet, isolated from other customers, from one booking of yours to the next (clause 6.2). We keep no copy of them anywhere else, make no backup of them and use them for nothing but serving you (clause 6.3).
  • Nothing goes to another AI service. Requests and data are processed only on machines we operate in Korea, and no request, prompt, output or other data of yours is sent to any other computing or AI service (clause 6.5). The only exceptions are transfers you ask for yourself, such as a download into your storage or a backup to your own bucket.
  • We never train on your data. We access it only as needed to run and secure the service, or where the law requires (clause 6.1).

2. When a fleet changes hands

  • Your working state, the cache and your storage on the fleet, is deleted when another customer's booking of that fleet starts, or 24 hours after your last hour on it, whichever comes first, or sooner when you ask (clause 6.2).
  • Before another customer's booking starts, the fleet's software is restored to our own image (clause 6.3), so nothing you ran, including a driver for your own model, can reach the next customer.
  • The sandbox host that runs code for your Functions is wiped at every change of customer as well.

3. Functions and the sandbox

  • Functions, and the tools your agent calls, that build, run or browse your code do it on a sandbox host attached to your fleet, never on the machines that serve the model.
  • The tools' indexes, build caches and dependency mirror are working state: kept while you hold the fleet, and deleted with the rest of it.
  • Each run gets a fresh virtual machine with no network access unless you allow it, and the virtual machine is destroyed when the run ends.
  • Fetch downloads only from the public internet over HTTPS. It refuses private, loopback and link-local addresses and our own hosts, and pins the address it checked. What it downloads is data and is never run; model weights are accepted as safetensors or GGUF, never in pickle formats.
  • A Function runs only when you start or schedule it, and what it produces is yours.

4. Keys and credentials

  • API keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes, never the key itself. Each key can be limited by scope, fleet, expiry and IP address, and revoked at any time.
  • A token for another service, such as Hugging Face, comes with each download request, is used only with the host it is for, is held in memory only while that download runs, and is never stored, logged or shown (clause 6.7).
  • Your repository is pushed to the fleet as a git remote, so we never hold a token for your git host. Daily backups are pulled by a scheduled command on your own machine with your own credentials, so we never hold those either.

6. Who can reach the machines

  • Only the people who operate SparkPipe can reach our systems, each with a personal key. Servers sit behind firewalls that block addresses after repeated failed logins.
  • They access stored data only to run the service, give support, keep it secure or meet a legal obligation.
  • The fleets are in premises we control in Korea. Our public server, which ends HTTPS and forwards traffic to the fleets through an encrypted tunnel, is in a data centre in Seoul.
  • All traffic to sparkpipe.ai is encrypted with HTTPS, and traffic between our servers runs through encrypted tunnels.
  • We keep access records for our systems and review them after any incident.

7. Incidents and outages

  • We tell you without delay of any intrusion, any leak of your information and any unannounced interruption of the service (clause 6.4).
  • Each node's cache and storage is mirrored to its pair partner, so a failed node loses neither. If an event destroys both copies of part of your working state, we tell you what was lost and credit 24 hours of the fleet (clause 6.6).
  • Downtime of more than 15 consecutive minutes credits every booked hour it touches, automatically, from our own records (clause 4.4). If your fleet cannot serve, we serve you on another free fleet without its working state, or credit the hours (clause 15.7).

8. Read what runs

  • The engine that serves your fleet is public at github.com/sparkpipe/sparkpipe, under the Functional Source License with an added permission. You can read it, build it and run it on your own hardware.
  • Some parts of the service run as separately licensed modules, which are not public. They run only on our machines in Korea and send nothing elsewhere (Agreement clause 7.8). Today there is one: memory, which keeps long conversations within reach for SparkPipe Chat and for API sessions that turn it on.
  • You can always leave with your data: export your storage, cache snapshots, draft model and adapters, and restore them to any fleet you hold, or take them home.

9. Reporting a vulnerability

Write to hyor@sparkpipe.ai with what you found and how to reproduce it. We reply, and tell you when it is fixed. Please do not test against fleets other customers hold.
