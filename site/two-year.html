Plan a term

What your term holds.

Each day in Korean time has three 8-hour windows: Americas 00:00–08:00, which is the previous day's working hours in the Americas, Asia 08:00–16:00 and Europe 16:00–24:00. Thirds guarantees one fleet in every hour. A 100% election puts all 24 daily hours into your own window, on up to three fleets at once, subject to our approval, and applies once the whole term is prepaid. We keep your guaranteed capacity off sale to everyone else until 6 hours before each hour, so you never need to take anyone's reservation, and no reservation is ever bumped.

The term covers every hour from Monday to Saturday: 15,000 to 15,048 hours, depending on the Sundays and 29 February in it. Hours exist only once paid; hours that pass before they are paid for are lost. A paid Tranche hour you have reserved but won't use can be released on the Hour Market before it starts: when it sells, or the shared pool uses it, you get hour-credits (HC) for what it earned, less a 10% fee, usable for 12 months.
