How it counts

  • Costs use each provider's published prices for its own API, checked on 2026-11-05: Anthropic (base input, 5-minute and 1-hour cache writes, cache reads, output, and Haiku 5.5's price above 100,000 tokens) and OpenAI (input, cached input, output, and the price above 272,000 tokens). Global endpoints, standard speed, no batch discount.
  • A request wrote its context again after a pause when it came more than the cache's lifetime after the previous request of the same session (5 minutes, or 1 hour for a 1-hour cache write; 5 minutes for OpenAI), its prompt had at least 10,000 tokens, and at least half of it was written or read uncached. "Saved had they been cache reads" is what those tokens cost beyond the cache-read price.
  • On a subscription you do not pay these prices, but the same tokens count against your plan's limits.
  • A SparkPipe fleet has no per-token price: a week is one flat price. With our engine installed from the Catalogue, its cache stays on the fleet while you hold it. To compare a team, add every member's logs.
