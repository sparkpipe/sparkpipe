Correction (2026-10-27, ../attention_rescale/summary.txt): the position-band and layer 35-40 localization above is the floor's own signature. Two more draws of the reference (c and d: the same numpy code and BF16 rounding, other FP32 reduction orders) score 0.648 and 0.639 at positions 550-1023 of design against A with B as the floor, the same as this gate, with the same per-layer onset. The "P split" variants were order noise (matmul2d keeps FP32 probabilities at full precision). What does separate Metal from the floor on all four prompts is the online softmax's eager rescaling: rescaling only past a margin of 8 lowers the paired statistic by about two standard errors at no cost.
