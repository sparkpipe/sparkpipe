## T0 pack production — ENOSPC incident + salvage (2026-08-24, MEASURED)

The original Phase 1 run died writing the final assembly copy
(`k3_pack.py:814`, `OSError: Errno 28`) at ~2026-08-23T20:21Z when spark6's
rootfs hit **exactly 0 bytes available** (3.94 TB volume). State found on
2026-08-24T04:43Z (fat-state capture, `tmp/k3_fat_state.txt`):

- `k3.full.tilek32.pack.payload` — 1,562,378,836,352 B, **COMPLETE**:
  `pack.close()` precedes the copy loop in `tools/k3_pack.py`, and the
  recovered manifest's `sum(bytes)` equals the file size byte-exact.
- `k3.full.tilek32.pack` — truncated at 650,314,252,288 B during the body
  copy; its header+manifest (350,620 B manifest, 2,157 tensors, zero
  misaligned offsets) are valid and were used as the salvage source of
  truth. Per-tensor interleave is tile_k 32 everywhere (row_bytes 16;
  w1 112 k-tiles × 384 cells; w2 96 × 224). The top-level `fmt.tile_k=128`
  echo in the manifest is an upstream cosmetic bug (module constant echoed
  instead of the `expert_tile_k` argument); per-tensor entries govern.
- Rank-pack symlinks 05–15 dangled: they were pre-created at 10:57Z, before
  production finished — the shard step ([2/3]) never ran, so rank packs
  never existed anywhere (no deletion involved). The `.payload.journal`
  present through 18:51Z (389,546 B) is gone now; resume-only artifact,
  logged as a minor open question.
- A plain restart was geometrically impossible: final assembly needs
  payload + pack resident together (~3.13 TB) while the checkpoint itself
  lives on extnvme (`extnvme/models/hf/moonshotai/Kimi-K3`) and every
  legal OUT_DIR layout (rootfs-only or extnvme-only) falls short of the
  simultaneous footprint; spark6's rootfs also sits at exactly 0 bytes
  free until salvage releases the dead weight.
