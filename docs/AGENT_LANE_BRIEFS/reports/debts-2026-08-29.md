### 3. The weightd PROT_READ prep: the scribble-probe receipt test

The W3 report's honest limit: consumer maps are RW
(`CU_MEM_ACCESS_FLAGS_PROT_READWRITE` in runtime/spark_weightd_attach.c)
and the read-only flip was "a one-constant change once a consumer-side
read-only receipt exists — left deliberately visible, not forgotten."
The receipt now exists at stub level:

- tests/cuda_stub/cuda_runtime_stub.c: `cuMemSetAccess` RECORDS the
  granted flags on the reservation (new `granted_access` field; zero =
  PROT_NONE while ungranted), and a new test-only probe
  `cuda_stub_vmm_probe_write(va, bytes, count)` models the driver's
  write fault in host form: a write through a span granted READWRITE
  lands; through a read-only or ungranted span it is REFUSED and
  touches nothing. Declared in tests/cuda_stub/cuda.h (marked "no
  real-CUDA counterpart"; the GPU receipt scribbles through its own
  mapping).
- tests/test_weightd_map.c (new section 7, registered in the suite's
  main): (a) the enforcement half on a raw stub mapping — ungranted
  probe refused, PROT_READ probe refused with the span untouched,
  PROT_READWRITE probe lands; exact teardown (unmap/release/free) keeps
  the stub's leak ledger green; (b) the production half — a real
  AttachPack + ImportMap against the in-process daemon, pack bytes read
  back byte-exact, then the probe through the REAL consumer mapping
  LANDS (the honest record: the consumer map is RW today), and after
  Release the same probe is refused (the mapping was the capability).
- THE FLIP CONTRACT (in-commit, in the test's doc comment): when
  runtime/spark_weightd_attach.c flips the one constant
  (PROT_READWRITE -> PROT_READ) on the GPU receipt, the production-path
  probe assert flips from CUDA_SUCCESS to CUDA_ERROR_INVALID_VALUE in
  the same commit — the refusal, not the landing, becomes the receipt.
  The test is the reason the flip will actually bite, and the ratchet
  that makes it visible the day it lands.
- All five weightd suites green: test_weightd (5 W2a contract lines),
  test_weightd_attach (both W2b suites), test_weightd_map (6 lines incl.
  the new one).

### 4. The batch-size knob: the 253-cap assertion

`SPARK_WEIGHTD_EXPORT_BATCH_MAX` (64) was already a named constant and
every use routes through it (daemon staging bound, wire control-buffer
sizing, client recv bound, reply validation) — what was missing was the
guard rail tying it to the kernel cap it must never cross. Now:
`_Static_assert(SPARK_WEIGHTD_EXPORT_BATCH_MAX <= 253u, ...)` in
include/sparkpipe/spark_weightd.h, citing SCM_MAX_FD — one EXPORT batch
is one sendmsg/recvmsg ancillary's fd payload; a value past 253 would
silently truncate fd delivery. Asserted, not folkloric.
